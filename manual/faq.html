FAQ

Connection and recording

The adapter is not found or will not connect
Make sure the adapter supports BLE (Bluetooth Classic-only adapters cannot be used). Turn on the engine (or ignition) and check that the nearby devices permission is allowed. It cannot connect while another app is connected to the adapter. For a Wi-Fi adapter, join its Wi-Fi network first, then enter its address (often 192.168.0.10:35000).
Recording does not start automatically
Only BLE adapters you have connected to from the app connect automatically when they appear nearby. Check that automatic recording is on in Settings → Recording. Battery saving settings that restrict the app in the background can prevent it from starting (set the app's battery usage to “Unrestricted” in Android settings).
The route (GPS) was not recorded
With location set to “Allow only while using the app”, trips that start while the app is closed cannot use GPS until you open the app or tap “Record GPS too” in the notification. To record from the start, turn on “Record GPS while the app is closed” in Settings → Recording. Trip details draw sections without GPS recording as a gray dotted line.
Some values such as RPM or fuel economy are missing
Values your car does not support are not shown (the app checks what is supported when it connects). In cars without a mass air flow (MAF) sensor, set the engine displacement for the vehicle to estimate fuel economy from intake pressure.
Will leaving the adapter plugged in drain the battery?
When the engine stops and recording ends, the app disconnects so the adapter can sleep (it stays connected only for the “Stay connected after stopping” time). Some adapters still draw power on their own, so unplugging it when you will not drive for a long time is safest. The app notifies you when a parked car's battery voltage stays low.

Fuel economy and score

The estimated fuel economy does not match reality
The estimate is calculated from mass air flow, or from intake pressure and displacement, and can be off depending on the car. Log your fill-ups to see the difference from your actual fuel economy; with Premium you can correct the estimate.
Is my driving score sent anywhere?
No. The score is calculated only on your device and is never sent to insurers or anyone else.
My score seems low
Every deduction is listed in the “Driving score” card in trip details, by event type and level. Check which events cost points. Events at levels hidden from the map (such as “Low”) are deducted too.

Data and privacy

Is where I drive sent anywhere?
Your routes (GPS records) never leave your device. There are no accounts or servers, no ads and no usage analytics. Only showing the map (the area shown is sent to the map provider) and showing start and end addresses (the device's address lookup) involve your location. If you turn them on, the current address (the device's address lookup) and weather on trips (positions rounded to about 1 km, sent to MET Norway) do too. See the privacy policy for details.
Moving to a new phone
Use “Export data” in Settings → Backup, then “Restore from backup” on the new phone.
Sending a trip to someone
In trip details, choose “Export” → “Share in CarReplotter format” to send it by a messaging app, email, Google Drive and so on. The recipient opens the zip file as it is, or imports it with “Import a trip” in Trips (there is no need to unzip it).
