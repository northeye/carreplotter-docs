Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy describes how the CarReplotter app ("the app"), developed by Northeye ("we"), handles your information. In short: the records of where you drove stay on your phone. The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics.

1. Information stored on your phone

The app stores the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Trips: GPS positions and their times, values read from your car through the OBD2 adapter (such as speed, RPM and temperatures), and the phone's motion sensor readings used to detect impacts
  • Information you enter: vehicle names, engine displacement, trip names, purposes, costs and memos, and saved places (such as "Home") with their positions
  • Information read from your car and adapter: the vehicle identification number (VIN) if the car provides it, and the names and Bluetooth addresses of adapters you connected to
  • Settings, and addresses of trip starts and destinations looked up as described below
  • The subscription status last checked with Google Play (whether you have Premium, and when it was checked)

We do not receive any of this information. You can delete trips and vehicles in the app; uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

2. Information sent outside your phone

The app does not send the records of your trips (your GPS track) anywhere. Only the following leave your phone, and only for the purposes stated:

Map display (Google Maps)

To show maps, the app uses the Google Maps SDK for Android. Map images for the area you are viewing are downloaded from Google, so Google receives the area being shown and technical information such as your IP address. Google's handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Addresses of starts and destinations

To show the address of where a trip started and ended, the app sends those two positions to your phone's address lookup service (the Android Geocoder; Google on most phones). Location information within the area of a saved place (such as your home) is not sent; the name you registered is shown instead of the address.

Current address on the meter and in the mini window (off by default)

Only if you turn on "Show the current address on the meter" and place "Location" on the meter, or turn on "Show current address" for the mini window, your current position is sent to the same address lookup service about once a minute while it is shown during recording (when you have moved 200 m or more), except within the area of a saved place.

Backups you set up

  • If backup is turned on in your phone's system settings, Android backs up the app's settings, vehicles, saved places and meter layouts to your Google account. Trip data is not included.
  • Trip data is written only to the files or folders you choose (manual export or automatic backup), such as a folder you specify on your phone or a folder in a cloud storage app.

Files you share

When you export a trip (GPX, KML, CSV) and share it, it is sent to the app you choose.

"Share in CarReplotter format" puts one trip (GPS positions, values read from the car and motion readings, the trip name and purpose, and, if you choose, the memo and costs) into a file and sends it to the app you choose. You can trim the start and end of the trip by the time you choose. The adapter name, VIN and addresses are not included. On the recipient's phone, the start and destination positions are sent to that phone's address lookup service to show their addresses.

Subscriptions (Premium)

Purchases, payments and cancellations of Premium are handled by Google Play. The app only receives the status of your subscription from Google Play (whether you are subscribed and whether it renews automatically); it does not receive your payment information, such as card numbers. The subscription status is not sent to any server of ours (we do not run one). Google's handling of this information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your OBD2 adapter

The app communicates with your OBD2 adapter over Bluetooth Low Energy or Wi-Fi to read values from your car. This communication stays between your phone and the adapter.

3. Permissions

  • Location (precise): to record your route. It is used only while recording, with a notification shown. If you choose to allow location "All the time" (optional, in Settings > Recording), the route is also recorded from the start when recording begins automatically while the app is closed. Even then, location is used only while recording, with a notification shown.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): to find and connect to your OBD2 adapter. Bluetooth scanning is not used to determine your location.
  • Notifications: to show that the app is recording or connected.
  • Companion device (optional): if you set up "Connect automatically when nearby", Android notifies the app when your adapter is nearby so that it can connect and start recording.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or share your personal information.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking.
  • The app does not require an account.

If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics with app developers in your phone's settings, Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash reports. These do not include your trips.

5. Children

The app is intended for drivers and is not directed at children.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available on this page, with the date it was last updated.
