Tested adapters

OBD2 adapters tested with CarReplotter by the developer or by users. Other ELM327-compatible adapters with Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) or Wi-Fi may also work.

  • Sleep: whether the adapter goes to sleep with "Put to sleep on disconnect" (AT LP) and wakes up when you start the engine. You can check it in the app from the adapter settings. Adapters without it may still sleep on their own some time after the engine is turned off.
  • Recommended (●): adapters that can sleep. They drain the car battery less while parked.
  • User reports: links to reports from users. The developer has tested every adapter on this list unless it is marked otherwise.
  • These are the results of tests by the developer and users. They may differ by vehicle, production lot or firmware version.

Tell us about your adapter

If you use an adapter with CarReplotter, please report whether it worked, even if it is already on this list. Reports are accepted as GitHub issues (a GitHub account is needed) and will be added to this page. Please don’t include your VIN, location or other personal information.
